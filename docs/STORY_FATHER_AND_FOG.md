# The Father and the Fog — story bible (archived proposal)

> **Archived.** This was the proposal that replaced the Port Vela / insurance-ledger story.
> [`STORY.md`](STORY.md) is the canon now; where this file and the scripts disagree, the scripts
> and `STORY.md` win. Kept for the reboot's history, not as a writing reference.

A simpler replacement for the Port Vela / insurance-ledger story. Same engine, same painted
world where it fits, same dry voice — one want, one obstacle, one twist.

## Logline

A cartographer's apprentice comes back to Dreadwater Isle to find his missing father. The only
captain willing to sail into the fog is the man everyone blames for the disappearances. The fog
is hiding something older than either of them.

## Tone

A fog-bound pirate adventure with dry humour. Scary where the fog is, warm where the island is,
theatrical whenever Vex is on screen. No bureaucracy, no insurance, no forms. The jokes come
from character: Vex's vanity, the islanders' superstitions, Fenn being the least piratey man on
the island.

## Backstory (what really happened)

- Dreadwater was a pirate port. Three months ago **Captain Elias Fenn** sailed into the Shroud —
  the permanent fog bank offshore — to chart a way through it. He never came back.
- Every ship that enters the Shroud vanishes. Trade is dying. The island is scared.
- **Captain Vex** is the only captain who still sails. He never enters the fog; he waits at its
  edge and salvages what floats out. The island blames him for the disappearances; he lets them
  believe it, because fear keeps his prices high.
- The Shroud hides the **Grey Lantern**, a pirate ship lost eighty years ago. Its captain,
  **Mordant**, refused to sink. It takes ships and crews to keep itself afloat. The fog is its
  wake.
- Elias is alive on a half-sunken rock inside the fog, charting the Lantern's patterns. He found
  the three rules that keep a ship alive in the Shroud.
- Vex saw Elias once and did not help him. He has been telling himself it was too dangerous ever
  since.

## The ghost (simple, gameable rules)

The Grey Lantern hunts by **sound** and **light**:

1. It follows a ship's bell. Silence the bell, and it loses you for a while.
2. It follows a lantern. Douse the light, and it cannot see you.
3. It cannot take a ship whose captain **speaks its name**. Mordant's name is the crack in the
   curse.

The crew it takes are the vanished sailors, still at their posts, not rotting but not living.
Freeing them (speaking the name, ringing the bell for the dead) breaks the fog.

## Four acts

### Act I — The Return

Fenn lands at Dreadwater. His father's room is untouched; the harbour is half-empty; nobody will
talk about the fog. He needs a ship and a crew.

Jobs (each is a character scene, not a fetch):
1. **The rudder.** Wreckers have taken Vex's spare rudder. Recover it by out-talking them, not
   fighting.
2. **Dorrit's name.** The cook is accused of selling ships' stores to the wreckers. Find the real
   thief and clear him; he joins.
3. **Quill's compass.** Vex holds her lucky compass as a debt. Win it back and she'll navigate.
4. **Marta's story.** Marta will lend the island's boat only if Fenn hears the whole story of the
   last ship that tried the fog — Rook's account.

Ends with: a ship, a crew of four, and Vex offering a deal — his chart of the fog's edge for a
share of whatever they find.

### Act II — The Crew

Finish fitting out; each crew member's reason for coming is shown, not told. First attempt into
the Shroud: the fog takes the light, the bell rings without a hand, and something enormous passes
under the hull. They escape. Vex's profiteering is exposed: he has been salvaging the lost ships
and selling the cargo back to the island. The crew wants to leave him; Fenn decides.

Ends with: Vex forcing the partnership — his ship is taken at the fog's edge, and everyone is
stranded on Fenn's smaller boat.

### Act III — The Shroud

Stranded with Vex, the crew at each other's throats. Vex admits he saw Elias. They find the rock:
Elias alive, half-starved, still charting. He has the three rules and a partial map of the
Lantern's route. The Lantern finds them; they escape by silence and darkness.

Ends with: the plan — silence the bell, douse the lantern, speak the name — and the knowledge
that the vanished crews are still aboard.

### Act IV — The Grey Lantern

Three objectives, in any order:
- **The bell** (Hobb): climb the Lantern's bell and cut it loose; his brother is the one ringing it.
- **The lantern** (Quill): douse the great lantern with the fog's own damp; a navigating problem.
- **The name** (Elias): find Mordant's name in the ship's old manifest/nameplate and speak it.

Then the choice: **break the curse** (free the crews, lift the fog — the island lives, but the
Lantern goes down with its captain) or **escape** (get everyone out alive, leave the fog and the
dead where they are). Vex's fate depends on whether Fenn trusted him: he stays to run the island,
leaves with the ship, or tries to take the credit.

## Reuse plan (keep the cost sane)

- **World:** Port Vela becomes Dreadwater Isle. The town, harbour, tavern, ship interiors and fog
  plates are reused with new names and new text.
- **Rooms:** dock = harbour; tavern = the Salty Keel; office = harbourmaster's house; warehouse =
  boat shed; chandlery = shipwright; village/alley = island lanes; Marrow decks/cabin = your ship;
  bow_fog = the Shroud; lagoon/reef = fog flats; salt_camp = castaway camp; weather_works =
  lighthouse; counting_house = Vex's warehouse; pier_end = harbour mouth.
- **Cast art:** Quill, Dorrit, Hobb, Marta, Bramwick, Rook, Nell, Tam, Onn, Ansel (as Elias) are
  reused. New art: Vex (portrait + body), Mordant (portrait), the Grey Lantern plate, a fog rock.
- **Systems:** verbs, sentence line, hints, chapter select, saves, endings, atmosphere, audio all
  stay. The casebook becomes **Fenn's chart** (same UI, new fiction); the deeds ledger becomes the
  **ship's log**.
- **Tests:** the playthrough is rewritten for the new acts; the fuzzer and invariants stay.

## What this costs

This is a story replacement, not a patch: acts.adv, every room's text and hotspots, ~30 dialogue
trees, tips, the two endings, the playthrough script, and four or five new art pieces
(~$0.30). The engine, UI, audio, scale and atmosphere work are untouched.
